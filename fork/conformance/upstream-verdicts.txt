# Which side is correct on each screen where upstream kmscon and the fork
# differ, for `make sheet-compare VERDICTS=fork/conformance/upstream-verdicts.txt`
# (docs/fork/conformance.md, "Comparison pages"). One line per screen,
# "screen: verdict"; it applies to every display the screen was drawn on.
# Judged from the tier 3 runs of upstream 7d33779 with libtsm v4.8.0 and the
# fork at 33e0ef0 with libtsm 06331b1.
sgr: The fork, on rendering rather than on any rule: both put the SGR 4 underline on the same pixel row of the cell, and the fork draws it at the text's full colour, where upstream draws it antialiased at about two thirds of it (158 against the text's 229 in grey) with a trace on the row below, so its line is fainter than the text it underlines. ECMA-48 (8.3.117) says only "singly underlined"; Ghostty and foot draw the line on whole pixels in the foreground colour.
colour-forms: The fork. ECMA-48 (5.4.2) makes the colon a separator inside one parameter, and xterm's ctlseqs documents 38:5:n and 38:2::r:g:b after ITU-T T.416; xterm, Ghostty, foot and WezTerm all colour these rows. Upstream's libtsm discards the whole sequence, so its colon rows keep the default colours and the last line's first half is plain. The fork's own defect shows here too: each full block (U+2588) it draws from the font leaves a dimmer pixel column at the cell's right edge, so the swatches show faint seams that upstream's do not, tracked in aaronsb/kmscon#46.
underline-styles: The fork. SGR 4:1 to 4:5 select single, double, curly, dotted and dashed underlines, sub-parameters by ECMA-48 (5.4.2), as Ghostty, foot, WezTerm and VTE draw them. Upstream's libtsm discards any SGR sequence holding a colon, so only the plain SGR 4 line is underlined, and the other attributes in those sequences (bold, red, reverse video, blue background) are lost with them.
underline-colour: The fork. SGR 58 sets the underline colour and 59 resets it, in the 38/48 colour syntax, as Ghostty, foot, WezTerm and VTE implement them; ECMA-48 leaves 58 and 59 unassigned. Upstream draws every underline in the text's colour, drops the colon rows' sequences entirely, and reads 58;5;196 as 58, then 5 (slow blink), then 196, a blink the tier 3 configuration hides with no-blink.
strike: The fork. ECMA-48 (8.3.117) defines SGR 9 as crossed-out and 29 as not crossed-out, and xterm, Ghostty, foot and WezTerm draw it. Upstream draws no strike at all, and loses the red curly underline of 9;4:3;58:5:196 to the colon.
boxdraw: Upstream. Since 39fd558 upstream draws the block elements U+2580 to U+259F itself, as hard-edged rectangles on whole pixel rows and columns, so neighbouring cells join without a seam, as Ghostty, foot and WezTerm draw them. The fork still draws them from DejaVu Sans Mono's glyphs: each full block leaves a dimmer pixel column at its right edge, so two in a row show a seam, and the half blocks and lower eighths end in a half-intensity antialiased row. The light, medium and dark shades differ in style only (upstream a flat fill, the fork the font's dither pattern), which Unicode's charts leave open. The fork's fix is aaronsb/kmscon#46.
wide-margin: The fork. A double-width character (UAX #11, East Asian Wide) that does not fit before the right margin wraps whole to the next line and leaves the last cell empty, as xterm, Ghostty, foot and WezTerm do with autowrap on. Upstream draws its left half in the last column and starts the next line with an empty cell in its place, in cases 2, 3 and 5.
combining: The fork. A base letter followed by combining marks is one extended grapheme cluster (UAX #29) occupying one cell, the marks having zero width (UAX #11, general category Mn), and xterm, Ghostty, foot and WezTerm draw the marks on their letter. Upstream drops the marks and shows the bare letter, so the marks column and the words row read e, a, n, o, A, c and "cafe manana".
vt-switch: Neither is wrong: both switch to tty3 and draw its login prompt. Upstream's prompt names the VT as well as the pseudo-terminal, "(pts/1 (tty3))", a change upstream made in 931c4a0, after the commit the fork last took from upstream (6c03eb4); the fork gains it on its next sync with upstream.
